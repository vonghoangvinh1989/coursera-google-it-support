+ wireless standard: IEEE 802.11 standards
    radio waves
    could be different frequency band
    most commonly 2.4 gigahertz and 5 gigahertz bands

+ frequency band
    a certain section of radio spectrum that's been agreed upon to be used for 
certain communications
    802.11b/a/g/n/ac/ax (wifi 6)
    802.11 --> physical and data link layers

    4 address fields
        address 1: source address fields
            mac address of ac that send the frame
        address 2: intended destination
            
        address 3: receiving address
            mac address of access point that should receive the frame
        address 4: transmitter address
            mac adress of whatever has just transmitted the frame
+ wireless access point
    a device that bridges the wireless and wired portions of a network

+ sequence control field
    16 bits long: sequence number to keep track of ordering frame
