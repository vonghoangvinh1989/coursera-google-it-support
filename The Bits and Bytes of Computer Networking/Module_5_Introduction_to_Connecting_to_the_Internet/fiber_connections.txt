fiber: using light to transmit 1 and 0
    FTTN: fiber to the neighborhood
        serve certain amount of population, after that use copper or something
    FTTB: fiber to the building
        data delivery to an individual building, twisted pair use inside building
    FTTH: fiber to the home
        run to each individual residence

    FTTB, FTTH: could be refered to FTTP (fiber to the premises)
