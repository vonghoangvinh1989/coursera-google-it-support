+ allow voice phone calls and data transfer at the same time and same line
+ use their own modem: DSLAM (Digital Subscriber Line Access Multiplexers)
    like dial up: set up data connection across phone lines
    unlike dial up: usually long-running, connection established when DSLAM is powered on

    type: ADSL, SDSL
        ADSL: different speeds for outbound and incoming data
            generally: faster download speeds, slower upload speeds (home users mostly need upload speed, they are clients)

        SDSL: symmetric digital subscriber line
            upload and download speeds are the same
