https: secure version of http (http over ssl or tls)

TLS
    a secure communication line, means data being transmitted is protected from potential
eavesdroppers

    the ability to authenticate both parties communicating, though typically only the server
is authenticated by the client 

    the integrity of communications, meaning there are checks to ensure that messages aren't 
lost or altered in transit

The session key: is the shared symmetric encryption key used in TLS sessions to encrypt data 
being sent back and forth

Pretty good privacy (PGP)
    an encryption application that allows authentication of data, along with privacy 
from third parties, relying upon asymmetric encryption to achieve this
